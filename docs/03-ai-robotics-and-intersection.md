## 📐 Mathematical & Algorithmic Approach to AI/ML

Alongside cybersecurity, I am studying AI-related subjects at university and want to develop a strong understanding of the **mathematical and algorithmic foundations** behind intelligent systems.

I do not want to treat AI as simply a collection of libraries or ready-made models. Instead, my goal is to understand the mathematics, algorithms, assumptions, and mechanisms behind the models I use:

| 🧮 Layer | 📚 Topics & Areas of Interest | 🔬 Mathematical & Algorithmic Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Mathematical Core** | • Calculus and linear algebra for machine learning<br/>• Optimization and gradient descent | • How **derivatives** contribute to **gradient descent**<br/>• How **optimization algorithms** minimize loss functions |
| **2. Core Machine Learning** | • Machine Learning (ML)<br/>• Regression and predictive modeling<br/>• Classification and model evaluation<br/>• Pattern Recognition | • How **regression models** learn parameters from data<br/>• How classification boundaries are formed and evaluated |
| **3. Deep Learning & Autonomy** | • Artificial Intelligence (AI)<br/>• Deep Learning & Neural Networks<br/>• Computer Vision & Natural Language Processing (NLP)<br/>• Intelligent and autonomous systems | • How **neural networks** learn representations<br/>• Underlying assumptions and mechanisms of intelligent systems |

> 🎯 **Current Stage:** I am building my foundations progressively, beginning with the necessary mathematics and core machine-learning concepts before moving toward more advanced deep-learning topics. My current goal is to develop genuine conceptual understanding rather than prematurely claim expertise in machine learning or deep learning.
