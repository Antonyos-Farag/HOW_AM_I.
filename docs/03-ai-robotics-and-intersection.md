# 3. Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Cybersecurity Intersection

## Mathematical & Algorithmic Approach to AI/ML

Alongside cybersecurity, I am studying AI-related subjects at university and want to develop a strong understanding of the mathematical and algorithmic foundations behind intelligent systems.

I do not want to treat AI as simply a collection of libraries or ready-made models. Instead, my goal is to understand the mathematics, algorithms, assumptions, and mechanisms behind the models I use:
- How **regression models** learn from data
- How **optimization algorithms** minimize loss functions
- How **derivatives** contribute to **gradient descent**
- How **neural networks** learn representations

### Core AI/ML Study & Interest Areas
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML)
- Deep Learning & Neural Networks
- Pattern Recognition
- Regression and predictive modeling
- Classification and model evaluation
- Optimization and gradient descent
- Calculus and linear algebra for machine learning
- Computer Vision & Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Intelligent and autonomous systems

> **Current Stage:** I am building my foundations progressively, beginning with the necessary mathematics and core machine-learning concepts before moving toward more advanced deep-learning topics. My current goal is to develop genuine conceptual understanding rather than prematurely claim expertise in machine learning or deep learning.

## Long-Term Vision: The Intersection of AI, Robotics & Cybersecurity

One of my main long-term interests is the intersection of AI and cybersecurity:

- **Academic Foundation (Robotics & CS):** Intelligent systems, algorithms, automation, sensors/actuators, and autonomous technologies.
- **Practical Foundation (Cybersecurity):** Networks, operating systems, security monitoring, endpoint investigation, and adversary behavior.

### Future Exploration Areas
*(Areas I aim to investigate and develop toward over time, rather than fields in which I currently claim professional expertise)*

1. **AI for Cybersecurity:** Applying analytical and automated techniques to defensive security workflows.
2. **Machine Learning for Security Analytics & Anomaly Detection:** Identifying deviations from baseline behavior across network and host telemetry.
3. **Intelligent Threat Detection & Investigation:** Enhancing alert triage and correlation using structured analytical models.
4. **AI Security & Protection of AI-Enabled Systems:** Securing machine learning pipelines, models, and deployments.
5. **Adversarial Machine Learning:** Understanding how attackers can manipulate, poison, or evade ML models.
6. **Security of Autonomous & Robotic Systems:** Protecting cyber-physical systems, sensors, actuators, and robotic controllers.
7. **Detection of Abnormal Behavior in Complex Systems:** Combining control theory, telemetry analysis, and security monitoring.
