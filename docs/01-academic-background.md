## 📌 Overview

I am an undergraduate student in **Computer Science and Information Technology at HNU in Egypt**, specializing in **Robotics**. My academic background combines computer science, robotics, intelligent systems, automation, and artificial intelligence.

Throughout my university studies, I focus on understanding how core computing systems, hardware-software integration, mathematical modeling, and security principles connect to one another.

## 🔗 Cross-Disciplinary Perspective

I am particularly interested in understanding how these academic subjects complement one another in practice:

1. **Operating Systems, Computer Architecture & Networks** provide the low-level foundation required for endpoint forensics, memory analysis, packet inspection, and system-level security monitoring.
2. **Algorithms, Pattern Recognition & Machine Learning** provide the mathematical and computational structures needed to model behavior and analyze data.
3. **Robotics, Embedded Systems & Automatic Control** provide insight into how physical and autonomous systems sense, process, and act upon their environments—and how those systems must eventually be secured against manipulation or abnormal behavior.
