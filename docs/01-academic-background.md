## Cross-Disciplinary Perspective

I am particularly interested in understanding how these academic subjects complement one another in practice:

- **Operating Systems, Computer Architecture & Networks** provide the low-level foundation required for endpoint forensics, memory analysis, packet inspection, and system-level security monitoring.
- **Algorithms, Pattern Recognition & Machine Learning** provide the mathematical and computational structures needed to model behavior and analyze data.
- **Robotics, Embedded Systems & Automatic Control** provide insight into how physical and autonomous systems sense, process, and act upon their environments—and how those systems must eventually be secured against manipulation or abnormal behavior.
